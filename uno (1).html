Rules

Goal

Be the first to get rid of all your cards.

Setup

Each player gets 7 cards. One card is placed face up in the middle.

Your turn

Play a card matching the color or number/symbol of the center card. If you can't, draw a card from the deck.

⊘ Skip

The next player's turn is skipped.

⇄ Reverse

The play direction reverses (with 2 players it acts like Skip).

+2 / +4

The next player draws 2 or 4 cards and loses their turn. With +4 you also choose a new color.

★ Wild

Can be played anytime, and you choose the new color.
